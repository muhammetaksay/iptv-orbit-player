IPTV Orbit Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Application version: 1.2.3

Application and publisher

IPTV Orbit Player is a free, open-source media player for Samsung Tizen TVs. It connects to Xtream-compatible services that the user is authorized to access. The publisher is Muhammet Kamil Aksay. The application does not provide provider channels, content subscriptions or media servers. The optional Hesapsız yerel örneği izle path plays its own bundled, MIT-licensed 120-second video without an account or Internet access. Every demo list row uses that same finite file; these rows are not real live broadcasts or a movie/series catalogue. The file has two synthetic AAC test-tone tracks and no embedded subtitles.

Privacy and support: aksay84@gmail.com · +90 505 014 95 55 · Support information.

Information stored on your TV

The application uses its local TV storage to remember automatic-login details and preferences you explicitly choose:

InformationPurpose and storage
Server address, username and account passwordSaved after successful login as akis.credentials.v1 for automatic login. These values are plaintext, not an encrypted vault.
HLS/TS format preferenceStored as akis.format.
Hidden/locked categories, picture aspect and seek stepStored as akis.preferences.v1. Category preferences are associated with a noncryptographic account-scope identifier derived from the server address and username.
PIN verification recordStored with the preferences using a random salt and PBKDF2-SHA256 derivation information. The PIN itself is not stored as plaintext. A category PIN is not a secure vault for the provider password or TV account.

The application does not persist viewing history, a recently watched list, search history or playback resume positions. Search results, programme-guide information and temporary category-unlock grants are held in working memory. Temporary playback state is necessary while the player runs and is not converted into a permanent viewing record.

Information sent over the network

The application connects directly to the provider you choose. Your server address, username and password are used to authenticate and request catalogues and programme information. Xtream API credentials may appear in the request URL query; media credentials may appear in the stream path. Identifiers for requested categories, streams or episodes are sent when needed. Search matching is performed within the application; the query you type is not sent to a publisher analytics service.

Channel logos, cover images, video, audio, subtitles, HLS manifests/segments/keys may be served by your provider or by third parties it specifies or redirects to. Those servers can see your network IP address, the requested resource and standard connection information. Your provider may retain its own records of requested media. The absence of a local viewing history does not mean that your provider or TV operating system retains no logs.

The application contains no publisher-operated analytics/telemetry, advertising, behavioural-profiling or application-account server. It contains no separate endpoint for sending your saved provider account to the publisher. Samsung platform services and your chosen provider apply their own data practices and terms.

Connection and local security

Use HTTPS when your provider supports it. Entering an explicit http:// address may use HTTP, which does not encrypt the connection in transit. The application does not silently downgrade a failed TLS connection to HTTP. Because credentials can be included in URLs, redact server details, usernames, passwords and media paths before sharing network-inspector screenshots or diagnostic records.

Because automatic-login details are plaintext, a person with access to your TV, application storage or developer tools may be able to read the saved account. Remove the saved account before transferring a shared TV to another person. No local PIN or software can guarantee absolute security.

Managing your information

  • Hesap → Hesabı değiştir / sıfırla (Account → Change/reset account) removes the saved server address, username and password. An existing PIN may be required. It does not clear all separate category, picture or seek preferences.
  • Use Düzenle (Edit) in the category screen to change hidden categories, remove locks or select PIN’i ve kategori kilitlerini kaldır (Remove PIN and category locks). An existing PIN is verified.
  • Change picture aspect and seek step in the player settings.
  • To remove application data completely, use the application/data-removal process supported by your TV. The available steps vary by model and software. Contact your provider about account data retained on its servers.

Saved login details may remain until you change/reset the account or the operating system removes application data. Separate preferences and the PIN verification record may remain until the corresponding settings are changed/removed or app data is removed. Resetting the account does not clear all these preferences. The application provides no automatic expiry period or service to erase local records remotely. Uninstalling the app is not a guarantee that every TV removes every record.

Support requests and policy changes

If you choose to contact the publisher by email or phone, the contact and message information you provide may be used to answer your request. Support messages may remain while needed to handle the request or meet applicable retention obligations, if any; no fixed automatic deletion period is promised. Do not send your provider password, PIN or personal media URL. A separate support-channel operator may process data under that channel's own privacy terms.

Use the verified contact channel at the beginning of this policy for privacy questions and requests. If the application's data handling changes, this policy and its update date will be revised. This policy covers the application; it does not control the store platform, your provider or technical logs maintained by the service hosting the policy page.
