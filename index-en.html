IPTV Orbit Player is a lightweight Xtream-compatible player for Samsung Tizen TVs. Connect your own authorized account to browse live TV, movies and series with your remote.

No provider channels or subscription are supplied.

The player interface is currently Turkish. Catalogue, programme guide, tracks and playback depend on your provider and TV.

Built for the living room

  • Live TV, movies and series
  • Programme guide and unified search
  • Audio, subtitles and picture controls
  • VOD seeking and numeric channel selection
  • Category hiding and user-created PIN locks
  • No persisted viewing/search history or resume position

Try the bundled offline sample

Choose Hesapsız yerel örneği izle to play the original MIT-licensed 120-second video without an account. All Live, Movie and Episode demo rows use that same finite file. It contains two synthetic AAC test-tone tracks and no embedded subtitles; it is not a provider broadcast catalogue.

Automatic login on your TV

Automatic-login details are stored locally in plaintext, not an encrypted vault. Requests go directly to your chosen provider and its media/image hosts. Read the full privacy policy before saving an account.
