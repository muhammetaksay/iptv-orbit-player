Open a support issue on GitHub

GitHub issues are public. A GitHub account is required. Do not include passwords, PINs, personal URLs or personal data.

IPTV Orbit Player is a free, open-source player. We do not sell channels, subscriptions or IPTV accounts. Contact your own provider about subscriptions, account activation, programme content or service outages.

When reporting an application issue, include your TV model, TV software version, application version, the screen affected and steps to reproduce the problem. Whether the stream is HLS or TS and a general on-screen error message may help. Do not send passwords, PINs, personal server/media URLs or unredacted network logs.

  • Playback will not start: Check the network and TV clock. Ask your provider to verify the account, simultaneous-connection limit and stream. An alternative live format may help in some cases; the application does not transcode unsupported codecs.
  • No EPG, audio or subtitles: The provider must supply the relevant data/tracks. Not every stream includes them.
  • Login requested again: After successful login, the account is normally reused from the TV's local application storage. Removal of application data may remove that account; uninstall/data-retention behavior varies by TV. Do not include credentials in a support request.
  • Seeking unavailable: Seeking is for movies and episodes; live time-shifting is not provided. Actual seek precision depends on the source and TV decoder.
  • Remote focus: Use arrows to move, OK to select and Return to close a window. In full-screen VOD, Down selects go-to-time/player settings. Arrows operate the virtual keyboard while it is open.

The in-app interface is currently Turkish.
